A Builder At Heart

I'm a second-year Computer Systems Engineer at the University of Georgia's Morehead Honors College, studying in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering.

As Chief Engineer of the MOCI mission at UGA's Small Satellite Research Laboratory, I direct the C-based flight-software architecture across five subsystems, building Docker-based development pipelines, writing C90 flight software with the Bright Ascension framework, and debugging embedded anomalies with oscilloscopes and encoder diagnostics.

I'm also an undergraduate researcher on a robotic gearbox disassembly project, designing finite-state machines for human-intent prediction and safety-critical path planning. Outside the lab I build whatever excites me: custom PCBs, embedded firmware, control systems, and computer vision pipelines. My philosophy: if it's worth building, it's worth doing right.
